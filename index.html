About the emblem. The red-and-gold striped umbrella above is the ombrellino (umbraculum); with the bell-on-a-pole tintinnabulum, it is the heraldic sign every minor basilica may display, recalling the canopy once carried over the pope. *Major basilicas (there are only four, all in Rome) are a papal category; every basilica in the United States is a minor basilica. Sources: GCatholic registry and Catholic News Service, cross-checked July 2026 (94 total). Site coordinates are city-level. Not affiliated with the U.S. National Park Service or the Holy See; the Unigrid layout is an homage.
